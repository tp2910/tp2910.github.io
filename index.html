Research

Computational and probabilistic methods for robust aerospace engineering design

My work develops computational and probabilistic methods that make aerospace engineering design more robust, efficient, and reliable — particularly for emerging sustainable technologies.

📊

Uncertainty Quantification

Propagating aleatory and epistemic uncertainties through high-fidelity aerospace simulations. Methods include polynomial chaos expansion, Gaussian process surrogates, Monte Carlo sampling, FORM/SORM, and Sobol sensitivity analysis. Application domains: hybrid-electric propulsion, structural integrity, and sustainable aviation fuel combustion.

⚙️

Multidisciplinary Design Optimization

Gradient-based and evolutionary MDO for aircraft systems. Robust and reliability-based design under uncertainty (RBDO / OUU). Integration of high-fidelity physics solvers (CFD, FEA) with MDO frameworks including OpenMDAO and GEMSEO. Adjoint-based sensitivity computation for computationally tractable multi-physics optimization.

✈️

Sustainable Aviation

Exploring the full landscape of pathways toward net-zero aviation: hybrid-electric and fully electric propulsion, sustainable aviation fuels (SAF) derived from biomass, waste, and synthetic routes, hydrogen propulsion, and operational efficiency improvements. My research quantifies the uncertainty and risk inherent in each pathway, supports technology selection decisions under uncertainty, and contributes to the development of certification and validation frameworks for next-generation aircraft.

🤖

Machine Learning for Engineering

Surrogate model development (Kriging, neural networks, Gaussian processes) for computationally expensive aerospace simulations. Physics-informed machine learning. Active learning for adaptive sampling. Application to UQ-MDO integrated workflows where evaluating high-fidelity models at every design point is infeasible.

🎯

Technology Maturation & Risk Analysis

Novel metrics for technology readiness assessment under uncertainty — Failure Impact (FI) and Uncertainty Impact (UI). Multi-attribute decision-making frameworks for prioritising component testing. Adaptive resource allocation for technology development programs, enabling rational test planning in the face of limited budgets and evolving information.
