Tavish Pattanayak

Incoming Assistant Professor of Mechanical Engineering

BITS Pilani, Dubai Campus  ·  Joining July 2026

I am an aerospace engineer whose research centres on making complex engineering systems more reliable and sustainable. Specifically, I work at the intersection of Uncertainty Quantification (UQ), Multidisciplinary Design Optimization (MDO), and sustainable aviation, developing probabilistic and data-driven methods that translate component-level uncertainty into system-level design decisions.

I completed my Ph.D. in Aerospace Engineering at the Georgia Institute of Technology (2025), where I was a member of the Aerospace Systems Design Laboratory under Prof. Dimitri Mavris. My dissertation developed a data-driven framework for resource allocation in technology maturation programs, with application to hybrid-electric propulsion systems.

Prior to joining BITS Pilani Dubai, I worked as a Machine Learning Engineer at Ursa Simulation, developing physics-informed surrogate models for engineering design workflows.
