About

I am an incoming Assistant Professor in the Department of Mechanical Engineering at BITS Pilani, Dubai Campus (joining July 2026). I completed my Ph.D. in Aerospace Engineering at the Georgia Institute of Technology, where I was a researcher in the Aerospace Systems Design Laboratory (ASDL) under Prof. Dimitri Mavris.

My dissertation, An Uncertainty Quantification-based Methodology for Resource Allocation towards Technology Maturation, develops a data-driven framework to optimise testing strategies for novel aerospace technologies — with a focus on hybrid-electric propulsion systems — by rigorously propagating component-level uncertainties to system-level performance metrics.

Prior to my faculty appointment, I worked as a Machine Learning Engineer at Ursa Simulation, applying physics-informed ML and surrogate modelling techniques to engineering simulation workflows.

My research lies at the intersection of Uncertainty Quantification (UQ), Multidisciplinary Design Optimization (MDO), and sustainable aviation — areas where rigorous probabilistic methods are essential for responsible decision-making in the design and certification of next-generation aircraft.

Research Interests

My work develops computational and probabilistic methods that make aerospace engineering design more robust, efficient, and reliable — particularly for emerging sustainable technologies.

📊

Uncertainty Quantification

Propagating aleatory and epistemic uncertainties through high-fidelity aerospace simulations. Methods include polynomial chaos expansion, Gaussian process surrogates, Monte Carlo sampling, FORM/SORM, and Sobol sensitivity analysis. Application domains: hybrid-electric propulsion, structural integrity, and SAF combustion.

⚙️

Multidisciplinary Design Optimization

Gradient-based and evolutionary MDO for aircraft systems. Robust and reliability-based design under uncertainty (RBDO / OUU). Integration of high-fidelity physics solvers (CFD, FEA) with MDO frameworks including OpenMDAO and GEMSEO. Adjoint-based sensitivity computation.

✈️

Sustainable Aviation

Electrification of aircraft propulsion systems — performance analysis, battery technology assessment, and certification pathways for hybrid-electric turboprops. Sustainable aviation fuel (SAF) analysis and alternative energy integration into commercial aviation roadmaps.

🤖

Machine Learning for Engineering

Surrogate model development (Kriging, neural networks, Gaussian processes) for computationally expensive aerospace simulations. Physics-informed machine learning. Active learning for adaptive sampling. Application to UQ-MDO integrated workflows.

🎯

Technology Maturation & Risk Analysis

Novel metrics for technology readiness assessment under uncertainty — Failure Impact (FI) and Uncertainty Impact (UI). Multi-attribute decision-making frameworks for prioritising component testing. Adaptive resource allocation for technology development programs.

🏭

Manufacturing Uncertainty

Uncertainty propagation in aerospace composite manufacturing processes. Variability in cure cycles, fibre orientation, and material properties and their impact on structural performance. UQ-informed design for as-manufactured geometries.
